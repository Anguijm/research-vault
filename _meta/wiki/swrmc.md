# Southwest Regional Maintenance Center (SWRMC)

A US Navy regional maintenance center at Naval Station 32nd Street, San Diego. Relevant to this
vault because it has built the Pacific expeditionary battle-damage-repair capability that
[[bdr-fleet-readiness-opportunity]] is aimed at, and because it coordinates that work through
[[srf-jrmc]].

## Facts

**FACT.** SWRMC's Executive Director is John Robinson, a 22-year retired Navy commander, in post
since May 2017, responsible for roughly 2,000 military and civilian personnel at Naval Station 32nd
Street and beyond. [s.mr26-clip10]

**FACT.** Robinson stated that repair tenders for the surface force do not exist and that no tender
plan exists for the foreseeable future. [s.mr26-clip10]

**FACT.** In place of tenders, SWRMC has pre-staged approximately 50 expeditionary maintenance
containers ("shops in the box") across the Pacific theater for battle damage repair on surface
ships. Containers either sit pre-positioned or move to an austere location, and are activated by
SWRMC sailors and civilians flown out from San Diego. The program currently supports single-ship
deployments of roughly two weeks. [s.mr26-clip10]

**FACT.** Pat McDermott drives the container program. Robinson did not describe his organizational
position. [s.mr26-clip10]

**FACT.** The container mission is unfunded. [s.mr26-clip10]

**FACT.** SWRMC sends project teams out for contract oversight and works through Ship Repair
Facility Japan Regional Maintenance Center, which Robinson identified as the Area Maintenance
Commander in the Western Pacific, to support the container deployments. [s.mr26-clip10]

## What is not established

Who owns the container mission. SWRMC built the capability and operates it into another command's
area, which makes it the most visible executor rather than a confirmed owner. No public source names
an owner. See [[pae-industrial-operations]] for why that gap is structural rather than an oversight,
and the capture brief's section 2.7 for how the opportunity treats it.

The division of work between SWRMC and [[srf-jrmc]] on the container mission and on the related
battle damage exercises is not described in any source held here.

## Also on record

Robinson announced the stand-up of Shore Intermediate Maintenance Activity (SIMA) San Diego, under a
December 2025 CNO mandate, with roughly 900 sailors moving from SWRMC's Production Department.
Captain Bill Albert is the commanding officer. Full separation runs through June 2029. Recorded here
because a new organization means a new contracting footprint. [s.mr26-clip10]

Robinson rejected "additive manufacturing" as a framing in favour of "advanced manufacturing",
meaning CNC plus additive plus reverse engineering, and named reverse engineering as the programme's
weak point. [s.mr26-clip10]
