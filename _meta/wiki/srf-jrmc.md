# Ship Repair Facility and Japan Regional Maintenance Center (SRF-JRMC)

The US Navy's forward-deployed ship repair activity at Yokosuka, Japan. It is the single entity that
appears in the most tracks in this vault: it is the customer set in
[[bdr-fleet-readiness-opportunity]], the subject organization in the world-class-planning study, the
coordinating command in [[swrmc]]'s container mission, and the operator's own workplace, which is
why the conflict-of-interest question attaches to it.

## Facts

**FACT.** SRF-JRMC is headquartered at Yokosuka and provides intermediate- and depot-level
maintenance to forward-deployed naval forces assigned to the Seventh Fleet, including destroyers,
amphibious warships, mine warfare vessels and logistics ships. [s.arec-ralphjohnson]

**FACT.** SRF-JRMC delivered USS Ralph Johnson (DDG 114) on schedule from a Chief of Naval
Operations maintenance availability on 6 April 2026. The availability integrated the ship's crew
with SRF-JRMC engineers, Japanese master labor contractors, local industrial partners and US-based
defense support companies. [s.arec-ralphjohnson]

**FACT.** Per SRF-JRMC, USS Ralph Johnson was one of seven US Navy vessels delivered on or ahead of
schedule between January and April 2026, including destroyers, mine countermeasures ships and an
amphibious transport dock assigned to the Seventh Fleet. [s.arec-ralphjohnson]

**FACT.** SRF-JRMC stood up a project team for the first Ship Wartime Repair and Maintenance
Exercise (SWARMEX) conducted in Japan. SWARMEX exercises give government Forward Deployed Ship
Repair Teams experience in battle damage assessment and repair, and develop partnerships with local
ship repair industry for maintenance resilience. They are conducted in the Seventh Fleet area of
responsibility. [s.navsea-swarmex]

**FACT.** The first Japan SWARMEX was executed on USS Fitzgerald (DDG 62). Mooring difficulties at
the Japan Maritime United Maizuru Yard pier forced relocation to the neighbouring Japan Maritime
Self-Defense Force Maizuru facility, and the maintenance was completed there. [s.navsea-swarmex]

**FACT.** Robinson of [[swrmc]] identified SRF Japan as the Area Maintenance Commander in the
Western Pacific, and said SWRMC works through it to support the expeditionary maintenance container
deployments. [s.mr26-clip10]

**FACT.** SRF-JRMC plans maintenance in AIM (Advanced Industrial Management), while the other
regional maintenance centers use NMD (Navy Maintenance Database). SRF-JRMC is not one of the four
public naval shipyards the AIM-NG process manual was written around. [s.aimng-grounding]

## What is not established

The division of work between SRF-JRMC and [[swrmc]] on the container mission and on the related
battle damage exercises. No source held here describes it, and the leading finding of the RAND
tabletop exercise is that battle-damage-repair command and control is ambiguous, so the absence is
probably the finding rather than a gap in collection.
