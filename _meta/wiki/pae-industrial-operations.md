# PAE Industrial Operations and the Portfolio Acquisition Executive restructuring

The Navy is moving most of its systems commands into program-aligned Portfolio Acquisition
Executives. This page exists because it invalidates a target the vault has been carrying: NAVSEA 04
is no longer where ship maintenance buying authority sits. It also explains why no source can name
an owner for the Pacific battle-damage-repair mission described on [[swrmc]].

## Facts

**FACT.** Roughly 70 percent of the functions and workforce at systems commands like NAVSEA will
transfer to the Portfolio Acquisition Executives, per Vice Adm. Seiko Okano, principal military
deputy to the Assistant Secretary of the Navy for Research, Development and Acquisition, speaking to
reporters in March 2026. The systems command continues in engineering, technical expertise and
infrastructure, while a large portion of the workforce and the mission authorities realign under the
PAEs. [s.usni-pae]

**FACT.** PAE Industrial Operations is led by Vice Adm. Jim Downey, who also heads NAVSEA, and
oversees maintenance and the public shipyards. [s.usni-pae]

**FACT.** PAE Maritime reached initial operating capability on a 16 July 2026 memo, led by Chris
Miller, running five deputy portfolios and overseeing the Supervisor of Shipbuilding offices at Bath
Iron Works, HII Ingalls and HII Newport News. [s.usni-pae]

**FACT.** One of those five is the Ship Readiness portfolio, led by Rear Adm. Andy Biehn with
civilian Lisa Bonacic-Doric, covering maintenance for combatants, the Arleigh Burke-class DDG 2.0
modification, training systems, and international support. [s.usni-pae]

**FACT.** A spokesperson for the Assistant Secretary of the Navy for Research, Development and
Acquisition told USNI News that a comprehensive Navy Systems Command study is underway and that the
study will inform the future roles and compositions of the systems commands. [s.usni-pae]

**FACT.** Other PAEs stood up are Undersea, Strategic Systems Programs and Marine Corps. [s.usni-pae]

## Why this matters across tracks

**Corrects a stale target.** Vault material written before July 2026 names NAVSEA 04 as a
systems-command contracting authority for ship maintenance. Maintenance and the public shipyards
have moved to PAE Industrial Operations. NAVSEA retains engineering and technical expertise, so
NAVSEA 05 remains correct as the technical authority for ship-design and damage-model data
questions. Anything naming NAVSEA 04 as a *buyer* should be re-checked.

**Explains an absence rather than filling one.** [[swrmc]] and [[srf-jrmc]] both operate a Pacific
battle-damage-repair mission with no identifiable owner in the public record. The organization that
would hold industrial ownership is being rebuilt now, with a study running to decide what the
systems commands become. Ownership is undetermined rather than undocumented.

**Names a plausible buyer.** The Ship Readiness portfolio holds maintenance for combatants and
training systems in the same portfolio under named leadership, and is new enough that its processes
and vehicles are being set rather than defended.
