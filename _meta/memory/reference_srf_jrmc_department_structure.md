SRF-JRMC command departments by code (operator-confirmed 2026-06-19). **There is no Code
400, 800, or 1000.**

- **100 — Command:** CO, XO, Executive Director, Legal, War Fighting Readiness.
- **200 — Engineering and Planning:** the CHENG ("Chang" in voice transcripts) plus engineers
  and planners. Owns the technical library, the derivative classifier (screens technical
  direction for releasability), and engineering/safety-of-life determinations.
- **300 — Waterfront Operations:** project managers who execute all the availabilities.
- **500 — Supply / logistics.**
- **600 — Financial:** budget and accounting.
- **700 — Crane and Rigging.**
- **900 — Production.**
- **1100 — Manpower:** training, manpower/HR, industrial security; manages the command's
  directives (SRF instructions).
- **1200 — Business and Strategic Planning Office (BSPO):** business agents performing the
  command's contracting functions. No contracting warrant officers at the command, so
  contracts go to an external contracting officer as a requirements package.

Use this for any SRF-JRMC work that needs the org. Full glossary lives at
`studies/ai-governance/_glossary.md`. Governing principle the operator stated for AI
governance: the AI changes no existing process — responsibility falls along these
established lines (each department is the SME/authoritative evaluator for its own domain).
