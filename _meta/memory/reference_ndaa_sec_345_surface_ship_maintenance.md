# 2026 NDAA Section 345 — Technology Enhancement for Surface Ship Maintenance

## What it is

Public Law 119-60, Section 345, signed 2025-12-18. Originated as S.1071 in the 119th Congress. Operator surfaced this on 2026-06-03 as policy context for the MegaRust 2026 trip-report Day-1 synthesis.

## What it requires

Per Holland & Knight's FY 2026 NDAA analysis (which is the operative third-party summary the operator can rely on without re-pulling the statute):

- The Secretary of the Navy **must investigate** advanced technologies capable of enhancing surface-ship-maintenance operations.
- **Adoption of identified technologies is mandatory** (not advisory).
- **Drone-based inspection for uncrewed structural surveys** is the specifically-named priority category.

## What it does NOT do

- No published deadlines.
- No published dollar amounts.
- No named programs or designated executing offices.

This is statutorily binding but lightly-specified. Implementation is at the Secretary of the Navy's discretion with respect to mechanics.
