# NAVSEA 05 named entities — coatings and surface preparation

These named entities were surfaced via ingested audio from MegaRust 2026 (NSRP SPC Panel, 2026-06-02) and confirmed by the operator on 2026-06-03. They are durable identifications useful across future trip-report processing, opportunity screening, and brief drafting in the Navy coatings / surface-preparation space.

## People

- **John McRory** — Plate Technical Warrant Holder at **NAVSEA 05**. The TWH on substrate / plate side for laser ablation and similar surface-preparation methods. Appeared virtually in MegaRust 2026 NSRP SPC Panel Q&A (clip 1).

- **Mark Ingle** — Coatings-side Technical Warrant Holder at **NAVSEA 05**. The TWH whose authority covers Navy coating specifications (including NSI 009-32 updates). Repeatedly intervened in MegaRust 2026 NSRP SPC Panel discussions (clips 2, 3, and 4 — in-room presence with multiple substantive contributions on wire-spray, R-value math, 8515 coating, NSI 009-32 anecdotes). Operator confirmed identity 2026-06-03; org confirmed as NAVSEA 05 (not 05P or 05P2 specifically — the sub-org detail was speculative on Claude's part).

- **Mark Lattner** — listed in the MegaRust 2026 agenda as NAVSEA 05P speaker (Wednesday morning slot). Distinct from Mark Ingle. Not referenced in clips 1–4 of the morning session.

## Standards bodies

- **AMPP** — Association for Materials Protection and Performance. Standards body developing the laser-ablation surface-cleanliness taxonomy (5–6 levels mapping to traditional SSPC SP-3 / SP-6 / SP-10 / SP-11 grades) referenced across MegaRust clips 1 and 3. AMPP is the post-merger entity that combined NACE International with SSPC.

- **SURFMEPP** — Surface Maintenance Engineering Planning Program. Operator-confirmed 2026-06-03. **Distinct from ST-1** — SURFMEPP and ST-1 are separate organizations. Whisper rendered SURFMEPP as "CERF" in clip 6.

- **ST-1 (Surface Team One) Corrosion COP** — Re-baselined Navy surface corrosion / sustainment program. New contract effective 2026-05-01 with 80 new hires brought on November 2025. Four new Knowledge Sharing Structures (KSS) added; aligned to the Carrier Team 1 operating model. Pacific footprint: Hawaii, Japan, San Diego. First ships are DDG 2.0 mods (East Coast + West Coast). ESC is Katie Buckley; sponsor is Captain Scott Tracy; Knowledge Manager is Steve Struthers; Kate Danny is the cross-coordinator with CT1. Monthly meetings every third Thursday. **ST-1 and SURFMEPP are separate organizations** (operator-confirmed 2026-06-03).

- **James Holman** — holds two jobs: (a) **PM of the ST-1 Corrosion COP**; (b) **separately employed by SURFMEPP**. Career background: Navy EMC retired 22 years + 15 years shipyard industry + 12 years at SURFMEPP.

- **Dr. Maureen Foley** — **ISEA (In-Service Engineering Agent)** for smaller composite components and polymer AM at NSWC Carderock. Operator-confirmed 2026-06-03 — title is ISEA, not ICA. 25+ years on this work track.

- **CT1 (Carrier Team 1)** — Carrier-side equivalent to ST-1. Also referenced as "CARES T1" by Whisper but the canonical acronym is CT1 / Carrier Team 1. Operator-confirmed 2026-06-03. Cross-coordinates with ST-1 via Kate Danny.

- **PMS 312** — NAVSEA Carrier program office. Operator-confirmed 2026-06-03. Funds composite bilge-box / bilge-strainer work for CVs (per Dr. Foley's clip 6 portfolio walk).

- **PCoE — Paint Center of Excellence** — Housed at NSWC Carderock. Takes input from SUBMEPP, SURFMEPP, SEA21, PEO Carriers, Fleet, and corrosion TWHs (Mark Ingle, John McRory) via a Project Advisory Panel (PAP). Funds: heated wire bend tables to all RMCs (Dr. Foley clip 6); deck-edge scupper production runs; corrosion-control toolkit distribution to Ships' Force. **PCoE is the program driving the MIL-PRF-16173 update to revision F** — the recent spec change Matt Chu referenced in clip 5 with Mark Ingle context. Operationally-significant cross-organization coordination forum for surface-ship coating and corrosion-control work.

- **SERDP / ESTCP** — Strategic Environmental Research and Development Program / Environmental Security Technology Certification Program. DoD's joint environmental-and-resilience S&T programs (with DOE/EPA). Whisper rendered as "CERDA PSTCP" in clip 5. ESTCP FY 2026 solicitation includes "Demonstration and Validation of Corrosion Mitigation Solutions." Relevant active project: WP21-5154 "Qualification and Demonstration of Oxsol-Free and Low-VOC Topcoats for Surface Ships and Submarines" (PI James Tagert, NRL; completed 2025) — the NRL-led sibling of the NSRP-funded 1K-polysiloxane-Oxal-free viability study in clip 2.

- **MIL-PRF-16173E → MIL-PRF-16173F update** — NAVSEA-driven via PCoE. E version cited 14 archaic Federal specs; F cites 1. Draft was out for industry comment as of early 2024. This is the spec-change Mark Ingle's office is shepherding. Affects Navy corrosion-preventive-compound work across surface ships.

- **FMMS — Fleet Maintenance & Modernization Symposium** — ASNE-organized, **Sep 22-24, 2026, Virginia Beach**. Sustainment-focused sibling to MegaRust. NSRP Sustainment Panel meets there.

## Standards / specifications

- **NSI 009-32** — NAVSEA Standard Item 009-32, the Navy-wide ship-maintenance standard item governing paint and coating application requirements. Appeared in Whisper transcripts as "W932" (the verbal "double-oh-nine-thirty-two" or similar). Updated whenever a Navy coating reformulates due to environmental regulations — this is the workload Mark Ingle's office owns.

- **MIL-PRF-23236** — Navy chemical-agent-resistant coating performance specification; Type 6 epoxy off-white is the variant referenced as the test bed in clips 1 and 3 ablation studies.
