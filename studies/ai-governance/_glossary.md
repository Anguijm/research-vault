# AI Governance Study — Glossary

Command-specific and study-specific vocabulary. The instruction expands AI-governance
acronyms inline on first use; this file is the durable reference for the SRF-JRMC command
structure and the recurring command terms.

## SRF-JRMC department structure (operator-confirmed 2026-06-19)

The command's departments by code. There is **no Code 400, 800, or 1000.** Each department
is the authoritative evaluator for AI-produced content in its own area of responsibility
(instruction §6.a); the AI does not change these established lines.

- **Code 100 — Command.** The Commanding Officer (CO), Executive Officer (XO), Executive
  Director, Legal, and War Fighting Readiness. *(Domain: command, legal, readiness.)*
- **Code 200 — Engineering and Planning.** The Chief Engineer (CHENG) and the engineers and
  planners. Owns the authoritative technical library and the derivative classifier, and the
  engineering authority for technical/safety-of-life determinations. *(Domain: technical/
  engineering.)*
- **Code 300 — Waterfront Operations.** The project managers who execute all the
  availabilities. *(Domain: availability execution, schedule, project management.)*
- **Code 500 — Supply.** Logistics. *(Domain: supply, logistics.)*
- **Code 600 — Financial.** Budget and accounting. *(Domain: budget, cost estimates.)*
- **Code 700 — Crane and Rigging.** *(Domain: crane, rigging, lift planning.)*
- **Code 900 — Production.** The production department. *(Domain: production.)*
- **Code 1100 — Manpower.** Training, manpower / human resources, and industrial security;
  manages the command's directives (SRF instructions). *(Domain: manpower, training,
  industrial security.)*
- **Code 1200 — Business and Strategic Planning Office (BSPO).** The business agents who
  perform the command's contracting functions. There are no contracting warrant officers at
  the command, so contracts go to an external contracting officer as a requirements package.
  *(Domain: business, strategic planning, contracting.)*

## Command roles and terms

- **CHENG** — Chief Engineer (Code 200). The technical authority for engineering and
  safety-of-life determinations.
- **CIO** — Command Information Officer. With the ISSM, custodian of the askSage platform
  (access, identity, the repository-connection registry, monitoring), not of department
  content.
- **ISSM** — Information System Security Manager. Runs Continuous Monitoring (ConMon) and is
  first response for AI security incidents.
- **BSPO** — Business and Strategic Planning Office (Code 1200).
- **Derivative classifier** — the Code 200 function that screens technical direction
  (drawings, policy, manuals) and redacts or denies unreleasable information before it
  reaches the share. The askSage knowledge base inherits this screening.
- **DFS** — Departure from Specification. The existing process for any deviation from a
  controlling specification (the JFMM, a NAVSEA Standard Item, a technical manual).
- **SORM** — Standard Organization and Regulations Manual. The command instruction governing
  records, directives, and information management; the instruction defers to it rather than
  restating those mechanics.
- **Flank Speed** — the Navy's enterprise Microsoft 365 / cloud environment that holds the
  command's CUI data and that askSage connects to.
- **ATO** — Authority to Operate. askSage is ATO'd at Impact Level 5 (IL5); the command
  operates under that ATO and owns the governance layer.
- **MLA / IHA** — Master Labor Agreement (formerly Master Labor Contract) and Indirect Hire
  Agreement: the employment vehicles for the command's Japanese Local National workforce.
