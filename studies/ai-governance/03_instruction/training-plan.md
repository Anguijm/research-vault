# SRF-JRMC askSage "Responsible AI and Use" — Training Plan

## 1. Purpose and gate
No user receives an askSage account until they complete this training (instruction §13). It is
**role-aware**, **recurring (annual and on significant change)**, and tracked in a registry
maintained by [the Department Training Coordinator / Code 1100 — FILL IN].

## 2. Tier 1 — General AI literacy (SOURCE from existing DON/DoD material)
Do not rebuild what the Department already provides. Per the March-2026 DON CIO direction
(reference (g)), assign from the existing free resources, for example:
- GenAI.mil platform training (DoD/CDAO).
- Navy LinkedIn Learning AI courses; Google Skills AI training; Microsoft Copilot training.
- Naval Postgraduate School AI course offering.
- The DoD CDAO Responsible AI material / GenAI Toolkit.

## 3. Tier 2 — Command-specific module (DEVELOP)
A short SRF-JRMC module (target ~30–45 minutes) that Tier 1 does not cover, built directly on
the instruction. Learning objectives — on completion the user can:
1. **Data boundaries (§4).** State what may go into askSage (CUI up to IL5) and what may never —
   **classified, NNPI, PII, PHI** — and recognize the aggregation/"mosaic" risk.
2. **AI is not an authority (§3, §6).** Explain that AI output is a non-authoritative draft, that
   the cognizant authority along existing lines evaluates it, and that "AI produced it" is never
   a defense for an error or a mismarked product.
3. **Technical authority & DFS (§6).** Recognize that anything touching a specification, tag-out,
   or seaworthiness routes to the CHENG chain, and that AI output is **not** by itself
   justification for a Departure from Specification.
4. **Out-of-band verification (§6.d).** Verify any AI-cited requirement against the controlling
   document through a non-AI source — never trust the AI's own snippet.
5. **CUI marking (§4.f).** Apply CUI designation/marking to AI output as the authorized holder.
6. **Export control / foreign disclosure (§7.c).** (Especially for Local National supervisors and
   sponsors.) No use case exposes export-controlled (ITAR/EAR) or NOFORN data to a foreign
   national through an agent; Foreign Disclosure and export-control processes still apply.
7. **Incident reporting (§11).** Know the split chains — spillage to the Security Manager/ISSM
   (and NCIS); safety mishap to safety/JAGMAN; rogue agent or suspected **prompt injection** to
   the ISSM.
8. **Resiliency (§12).** Know the workflow's documented manual fallback.

## 4. Required exercise — "show a real hallucination" (§13.c)
The module **demonstrates a real AI error** during onboarding — e.g., the AI confidently
inventing a torque value, a NAVSEA Standard Item paragraph, or a citation that does not exist —
to inoculate users against automation bias. This is a live demonstration, not a slide.

## 5. Role-aware add-ons
- **Oversight roles** (CIO/ISSM, CHENG-chain evaluators, Department Heads, designated
  authoritative AI content evaluators): an additional governance module on their
  instruction responsibilities (use-case approval, evaluation, monitoring, the departmental
  plan).
- **Designated evaluators:** the out-of-band verification procedure in depth.

## 6. Assessment, attestation, and tracking
- Short knowledge check on the Tier-2 objectives; a user attestation acknowledging the data
  boundaries and the accountability rule (§3.b).
- Completion (Tier 1 + Tier 2) recorded in the training registry; it is a precondition for
  account provisioning (§13). Annual refresher; re-train on significant change.
