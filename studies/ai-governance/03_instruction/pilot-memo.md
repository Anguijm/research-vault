**Subj:** AUTHORIZATION OF A LIMITED PILOT OF THE ASKSAGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM

**Ref:**
- (a) Draft SRF-JRMC Instruction 5239.1, Governance, Security, and Use of the askSage Artificial Intelligence Platform (in working-group review)
- (b) DON CIO Memorandum, Enterprise IT Service Designation for GenAI.mil (28 January 2026)
- (c) DON CIO Memorandum, Accelerating AI Adoption / Measuring Time Savings (March 2026)

**Encl:**
- (1) Departmental Information-Governance Plan (template)

1. **Purpose.** This memorandum authorizes a limited, time-bounded pilot of the askSage
artificial intelligence (AI) platform. The pilot exercises the governance framework of
reference (a) *before* that instruction is promulgated, in order to (a) validate the framework
with real command work, (b) generate measured time-savings data consistent with reference (c),
and (c) surface lessons that finalize reference (a) and document the command's justification for
askSage as a separately-authorized, mission-specific agentic capability.

2. **Background.** The command employs both the Department of the Navy enterprise platform
GenAI.mil and the askSage platform, using GenAI.mil for general questions and askSage for
agentic work, consistent with references (a) and (b). Reference (a) establishes the command's
askSage governance and is in working-group review. A limited pilot under that draft framework
lets the command prove the controls, measure the benefit, and refine the instruction before it
is signed.

3. **Scope.** The pilot is restricted to **low-risk, high-value, read-only** use cases. No
write-enabled, autonomous, or higher-risk use is authorized during the pilot.
   a. Participating departments (codes): [FILL IN].
   b. Pilot use cases (one or two per participating code): [FILL IN].
   c. Pilot period: 90 days from the start date, [FILL IN start–end], unless extended or
      terminated in writing.

4. **Governance applies in full.** The pilot operates under the controls of reference (a). In
particular:
   a. **Pre-access gates.** Before its personnel receive pilot access, each participating
      department completes the departmental information-governance plan (enclosure (1)), and each
      user completes the "Responsible AI and Use" training (reference (a), Sections 13 and 15).
   b. **Data boundaries.** Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) up to Impact Level 5 only.
      **No classified information, Naval Nuclear Propulsion Information (NNPI), Personally
      Identifiable Information (PII), or Protected Health Information (PHI)** is to be entered
      into askSage (reference (a), Section 4).
   c. **Human-in-the-loop.** All AI output is a non-authoritative draft, authoritatively
      evaluated by the cognizant authority along existing lines of responsibility (reference (a),
      Section 6). Anything bearing on a technical or seaworthiness determination is adjudicated in
      the Chief Engineer's chain.
   d. **Resiliency.** Every pilot workflow has an immediately-available manual fallback
      (reference (a), Section 12).
   e. **Monitoring, records, incidents.** Per reference (a), Sections 9 through 11, including the
      split incident-reporting chain (security spillage to the Security Manager and ISSM; safety
      mishaps through safety/JAGMAN channels).

5. **Metrics and time-savings tracking.** Each pilot use case establishes a manual (baseline)
task time and tracks baseline-versus-AI-assisted task time, reporting the result through the
department's plan and the governance forum, consistent with reference (a), Section 9.e, and
reference (c). The command also tracks, for the pilot: security and spillage incidents (target:
zero), the effectiveness of the designated-evaluator sign-off, validation of the manual
fallback, and user adoption.

6. **Responsibilities.**
   a. **Command Information Officer (Code 109) and Information System Security Manager (ISSM):**
      provision pilot access and identity, run platform monitoring, support participating codes,
      and consolidate the pilot reports in paragraph 7.
   b. **Chief Engineer (CHENG) and chain:** perform the technical/functional evaluation of any
      pilot output touching their domain.
   c. **Participating Department Heads:** propose the pilot use cases, designate the authoritative
      AI content evaluator, complete the departmental information-governance plan, enforce the
      controls in paragraph 4, and track time savings (paragraph 5).
   d. **Governance forum (Department Heads):** review pilot progress and results.

7. **Reporting and decision gate.** The Command Information Officer will provide an interim
report at the pilot midpoint and a final pilot report at completion to [the Executive Director /
the undersigned] and the working group. The final report will address the metrics in
paragraphs 5 and recommend whether to promulgate reference (a) and proceed to phased expansion,
with any adjustments the pilot indicates.
