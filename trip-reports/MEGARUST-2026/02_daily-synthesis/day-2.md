## Day-2 executive takeaways

These are the items an executive (CACI or SRF-JRMC) needs to know from Day 2.

### 1. Vice Admiral Lewis's PAE acquisition reform is the structural Day-2 policy frame.

Vice Admiral Dave Lewis (ASNE President) opened the day with a direct response to the Day-1 acquisition-sustainment feedback-gap pattern. Per Lewis, the new Portfolio Acquisition Executive (PAE) construct aligns the buyer and sustainer of Navy assets in the same organization — "the buyer of the stuff is not the same organization. It's the maintainer of the stuff. Part of the reform is to create portfolios where the buyer and the sustainer are the same human being." His direct instruction to the audience: "Go find your PAE and make nice with that person — they now are the solution to our corrosion issues." The PAE owns contracting, engineering, and management in one place. [Source: clip 7, 04:18–06:51.]

### 2. The Anti-Rust Program / SWARM Initiative is post-launch with concrete deployment numbers.

Mark Lattner (NAVSEA 05P SES) reported that the Anti-Rust Program kicked off January 2026. Numbers since kickoff: **85 availabilities** hit, **~60,000 square feet of preservation** completed, **3,000+ AM parts** deployed to ships (Lattner's framing: "more AM components on Navy ships than everyone else combined"). The program runs on two parallel tracks — CCAT (traditional sailor-training, 16-year history, 9 worldwide locations) and CCAT Prime / SWARM Teams (contractor-led production work at short availabilities). Lattner explicitly acknowledged the Day-1 Ian Shannon "we call it crack" workforce-civilian-ratchet observation as a managed risk. [Source: clip 7 Lattner segment.]

### 3. Mark Ingle (NAVSEA 05P2) is the coatings TWH at the apex of the Technical Authority Pyramid.

The Technical Authority Pyramid slide is the canonical NAVSEA 05 coatings TWH structure: Mark Ingle ("Ingle: all") at the top, covering all product codes. Howard Castle as acting non-metallic materials TWH and acting EM for corrosion. Nate Livesey as a new acting EM. DRP-driven vacancies are structural — three of four named MegaRust contacts (Nate Livesey, Bob Steele, Kylee Fazende) hold "Acting" titles, and multiple pyramid positions show "?" for unfilled slots. NSWC Carderock is institutional home of PCoE (Kylee Fazende, Acting PM). [Source: clip 7 Ingle Technical Authority Pyramid segment.]

### 4. Standard Item 009-032 FY-28 Change 1 was published 31 March 2026.

49 of 79 proposals adopted (~60%). HQ-NAVSEA review process: SEA 04X, 05D, 05P, 05PV, 05U, 05Z, PMS 321, PMS 421, SEA 08. Top 5 updates: zinc-rich paints (MIL-DTL-24441 Type 3 Formula 159), MIL-PRF-24647E foul-release antifouling Type IV biocide-boosted (alternative to copper), Paragraph 3.1.16 streamlined waterfront process for interior topcoats, magazine/ammunition-hold coating Note (12), and fluidized-bed powder coating expanded to airtight/watertight doors, hatches, scuttles. [Source: clip 7 Ingle 009-032 segment.]

### 5. The Day-1 clip-5 Matt Chu / Mark Ingle "huge conversation" thread is now fully on-tape concrete.

Mark Ingle disclosed that the MIL-PRF-16173F publication was held from August 2025 to late May 2026 by a NAVAIR-vs-NAVSEA format dispute. NAVAIR (Matt Chu's office) came in late August 2025 saying they couldn't accept the new Type/Class/Grade format because it would force expensive downstream document updates. Resolution: revert to Class/Grade format, minimize NAVAIR-side impact. Class 8 "Persistent" preservatives is the new MIL-PRF-16173F addition (originally proposed as Class 9). Concurrence received about one week before MegaRust. [Source: clip 7 Ingle 16173F segment.]

### 6. The Day-1 clip-2 "Oxal / Oxyl" thread is now fully resolved with on-tape chemistry naming.

Mark Ingle: "Oxsol is parachlorobenzyl trifluoride." Oxsol-100 is a non-VOC solvent used in MIL-PRF-24635F Type V coatings; California listed it as a Proposition 65 carcinogen in 2019; the South Coast Air Quality Management District is changing Rule 1106 (Pleasure Craft and Marine) to phase it out. NRL is leading the reformulation work via ESTCP project WP21-5154 (PI James Tagert), with a qualification package in progress. [Source: clip 7 Ingle Oxsol segment.]

### 7. CAPT Duldulao reframed corrosion as a combat-systems warfighting threat with concrete cost data.

NSWC PHD Chief Engineer (MIL) CAPT Richard Duldulao opened with a structural reframe: "When a part of the [combat] system degrades, [the] kill chain potentially weakens." On-tape cost data: **$14M per SPY-1 array replacement** (three arrays per ship), **$450K per FCS MK 99 director replacement**, **$500K per AEM/S resurfacing**. Named hulls with FCS director corrosion replacements during recent availabilities: **USS Wayne E. Meyer (DDG-108) and USS Cole (DDG-67)**. **FY25 INSURV documented ~900 deficiencies attributed directly to corrosion in combat-systems inspections, of which over 660 degraded mission capability**. The "few seized train bearings in the active fleet" detail (RAM launchers physically unable to slew) is on tape only, not on the slide. [Source: clip 8 Duldulao keynote.]

### 8. NSWC PHD uses a different coating-spec track for combat-systems composite structures.

Tim Tenopir (NSWC PHD OOT — Office of Engineering and Technology) disclosed that NSWC PHD has moved away from MIL-PRF-23236 and MIL-PRF-24635 polysiloxane on combat-systems composite structures (AEM/S etc.) in favor of "85285 polysiloxane formula or polyurethane" (likely MIL-PRF-85285). This is a separate spec-track from Mark Ingle's HM&E topside polysiloxane work (clip 7) — the two coatings authority lanes don't fully overlap. [Source: clip 8 Tenopir Q&A response.]

### 9. Technical Track #4 is the Anti-Rust Program's industry-evaluation forum.

Nate Livesey moderated Track #4 (Anti-Rust PM Acting, NAVSEA — same person on Lattner's team per clip 7). Four industry presentations across two paired themes: **Robotics for inspection** (SwRI Samet on state of the art and the "data-to-action gap" framing; Edify Apt on wet-dock magnetic-crawler NDI with PECA + ultrasonic, citing CBO analysis of 14 years of yard-period data) and **Advanced coating application** (Apellix Kunkle on spray-painting drones — same person and 30-minute deep dive of the Day-1 clip-4 pitch; PPG McCarthy on electrostatic coating application, pinch-hitting for Bart Dima). Operator can decide whether any of these vendors are CACI BD-relevant. [Source: clip 9 entire track.]
