## Summary (provisional — from slides only)

NSWC PHD frames the briefing as **the warfare-systems-side counterpart to the morning HM&E-focused coatings discussion**. The argument: combat systems require different sustainment approaches than hull, mechanical, and electrical (HM&E) systems, and FY25 INSURV documented over 660 corrosion-related deficiencies that degraded mission capability. The briefing walks through material-degradation examples on each major surface-ship combat system family — AEGIS, NATO Sea Sparrow Missile System (NSSMS), Rolling Airframe Missile (RAM), AEM/S (Advanced Enclosed Mast/Sensor), and Underway Replenishment (UNREP) equipment — then surfaces NSWC PHD's current initiatives across training, tech standards, collaboration, and inventions/patents.

The substantive program-level payload is the **NSSMS/RAM On Ship Refurbishment Program (OSRP)** cost-comparison slide showing 30-year cost-savings against the alternative Depot Overhaul (OH) pathway. Multiple NAVSEA tech docs are cited including TM/TWITS documents for combat system coating system refinishing procedures for DDG 173 class and DDG 172 class, and antenna and radome field MCA inspection guidance.

(`p_nssms-ram-osrp.jpg`)

| Unit | Avg Years Between (OSRP / OH) | Cost ($M) (OSRP / OH) | Cumulative Cost Over 30 Yrs (OSRP / OH) | Savings for OSRP | 30 Year Savings — CVN | LHA/LHD | LPD/LSD |
|------|-------------------------------|-----------------------|------------------------------------------|------------------|-----------------------|---------|---------|
| N Director | 5.9 / 12 | $0.340 / $0.92 | $1.729 / [?] | 0.439 | $1.757 | $0.878 | $0 |
| N Launcher | 5.7 / 12 | $0.400 / [?] | $2.105 / [?] | 1.146 | $2.293 | $2.293 | $0 |

| **30-Year Savings TOTAL by ship class** | | | | | **$25.953** | **$25.075** | **$21.904** |

CVN configuration: 4 N Directors, 2 N Launchers, 2 RAM Launchers
LHA/LHD: 2 N Directors, 2 N Launchers, 2 RAM Launchers
LSD/LPD: 2 RAM Launchers

Legend:
- **OH = Depot Overhaul (1 year to complete)**
- **OSRP = On Ship Refurbishment Program (3-6 weeks to complete)**

This is the program-level cost-savings argument: OSRP runs ~5-6 years between cycles at $300-400K per cycle, vs. OH at ~12 years between at higher cost. 30-year cumulative savings: $25.9M per CVN, $25.1M per LHA/LHD, $21.9M per LPD/LSD. The dramatic LSD/LPD savings come from RAM Launcher OSRP applied to a class that wouldn't otherwise see N Director / N Launcher work.

The schedule delta is operationally significant: **OSRP takes 3-6 weeks vs. OH at 1 year**. Compatible with short availabilities that ship classes increasingly use rather than full depot periods.
