# MegaRust 2026 — NSRP SPC Panel: Five back-half project pitches

## Summary

This clip captures the back half of the morning NSRP SPC Panel session — five short project pitches presented back-to-back after the Pulse Laser Comparison Tool report in clip 3. The format is "pitch + Q&A + next pitch," each running 5 to 10 minutes. Two NSRP-affiliated industry firms (DCI Defense Group and Apellix/Cro-Co-Tec) presented vendor-pitch-style projects looking for shipyard partners or NSRP sponsorship. Two pitches came from the same NSRP-funded research team that also presented clips 1, 2, and 3 (LZ Technology / Elzly Technology), extending their work into plasma blasting and into wire-spray thermal-spray practices. The closing pitch is a re-pitch of a wait-listed Thermal Insulating Coating (TIC) shipboard-demonstration project.

A notable moment in the clip: **Denny's atmospheric-plasma status report during the plasma-blasting pitch.** Atmospheric plasma is "fully approved and being deployed within the Navy" on ships at sea, including down to within three inches of submarine pressure hulls. Passed all tech-warrant-holder requirements and fatigue tests. The Corrosion Control Repair Technology Focus Team (CCR TFT) led by Conlan Hsu and James Brooks is referenced as the alignment forum for NSRP panel projects.
