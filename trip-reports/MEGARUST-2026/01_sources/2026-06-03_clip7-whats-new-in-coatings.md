# MegaRust 2026 — NAVSEA SEA 05P2 "What's New in Coatings?" (Day 2 morning)

## Summary

The briefing followed the Day-2 agenda Dave Zilber outlined at the end of Day 1: morning track is "What's New In Coatings?", afternoon is the Nonskid Working Group interactive discussion (Standard Item 009-032 updates, Standard Item 009-124 Thermal Spray Nonskid updates, new nonskid qualification opportunities). The briefing slide deck framing slide explicitly states "**SEA 05P2 seeking input from technical community**" and emphasizes the interactive format ("avoid one-way presentations regarding new materials / requirements").

Five major content blocks visible from the slides:

1. **NAVSEA SEA 05 organizational structure** (Draft April 2026 org chart) + **Technical Authority Pyramid for Coatings & Corrosion Control** (Draft April 2026). Mark Ingle is at the top of the pyramid as the singular TWH covering all product codes.
2. **NAVSEA Anti-Rust Program / SWARM Initiative** (Swift Warship Action on Rust Mitigation) — corrosion control on warships. Two-fronts framing: CCAT (traditional — training, tools, technical expertise, technology transition) + CCAT Prime / C 2 Prime — SWARM Teams (production work, rapidly employing new tech on ships). Dozens of ships worked.
3. **Paint Protective Film (PPF)** — new product approach to running rust at deck edges. Slide-shown 4-month results: no running rust with film installed; visible rust streaks where film was not installed.
4. **Standard Item 009-032 — FY-28 Change 1** published 31 March 2026 by Commander, Regional Maintenance Center. 79 total proposals processed (9 adopted / 30 adopted-modified / 35 not adopted / 5 other). Top Five updates include zinc-rich coating option (MIL-DTL-24441D Type III Formula 159), MIL-PRF-24647E foul-release coating requirements, paragraph 3.1.16 streamlined waterfront process, deck-coating Table 2 updates with new note 12 on magazine/ammunition holds, and fluidized-bed powder coating list expansion.
5. **Specification updates announced:**
   - **MIL-PRF-16173F** — adds new Class 8 "Persistent" corrosion preventive compounds for spaces where coating installation/maintenance is impractical. Technical community input requested.
   - **MIL-PRF-24712B Amendment 1** — published 28 December 2025; powder coating specification update with color requirements migrated from cancelled FED-STD-595 to active SAE AMS-STD-595.
   - **Industry Day July 14-15 (Norfolk VA)** — Anti-Rust Team interactions with demonstrations. POC Josh Chapman, NCMS.

The briefing is operationally adjacent to the operator's SRF-JRMC work in several ways: SEA 05P2 is Mark Ingle's office (per cross-clip pattern from Day 1); the Anti-Rust Program / SWARM Initiative is the cross-fleet corrosion-control program structure; PPF and the 009-032 deck-coating updates affect what surface-prep work happens on DDGs at SRF-JRMC; and the MIL-PRF-16173F Class-8-Persistent-Preservative work has direct application to spaces SRF-JRMC encounters routinely.

### Slide p11 — "What's new with Standard Item 009-032?" Top Five updates (#1–3)

(`p11_009-032-top-five-1-3.jpg`)

1. **Updated Table 2, Lines 1A and 1B, and Table 5, Line 27** — provides an option to use the **MIL-DTL-24441D, Type III qualified, Formula 159, zinc-rich coating** on freeboard, topside, and Jet Blast Deflector (LBD) pits. Zinc-rich coating retards undercutting and rust staining at defects or holidays.

2. **Updated Table One, Lines 3, 6, 18 and Lines 14, and 22** — defines requirements for use of **MIL-PRF-24647E qualified, Type IV, biocide-boosted, foul-release coatings** on steel, aluminum, or GRP substrates for up to seven years of service. Three coats of foul-release topcoat support up to seven years of service with two coats supporting up to two years of service.

3. **Updated paragraph 3.1.16** to include a new clause addressing the streamlined waterfront process of applying two coats of interior topcoats to effectively cover or hide the substrate primer by adding the following: "With the exception of MIL-PRF-24596, MIL-DTL-15090, and MIL-DTL-24607, when applying paint, multiple coats must be of contrasting colors, unless specifically stated in Tables One through 8."

Bottom statement: "**FY-28, Change 1, Standard Item 009-032 enhances coating corrosion-control performance, addresses Fleet needs, and reduces life-cycle maintenance costs.**"

### Slide p22 — "Opportunity for Application of future, Class 8 'Persistent' Preservatives"

(`p22_persistent-preservatives-class-8.jpg`)

**STATUS:** Updated MIL-PRF-16173F specification includes new Class, Grade, and Application requirements, including **"Class 8, Persistent"** corrosion-preventive compounds intended as **supplement for coatings in spaces where coating installation and maintenance is impractical and corrosion is an issue.**

**Key technical considerations for use of "persistent" preservatives:**
- Space or void where coatings are impractical to install that are analogous to interior of rudder or bilge keel, but more accessible to the environment or weather.
- Cosmetic appearance of waxy, oily coating will not be an issue. Translucent, amber preservative can attract dirt or dust.
- Thick, waxy material will not interfere with mechanical equipment operation.
- Runoff or release of preservative to the environment will not be significant.

**TECHNICAL COMMUNITY INPUT NEEDED ON KEY ISSUES:**
1. Define quality control requirements for acceptable, and unacceptable, preservative application process. April 2024 — Shipyard demonstration still controlling corrosion.
2. Shipyard process validated UV light inspection, process controls, and clean-up.
3. Need to define applicable spaces and areas for preservatives, possible new **SURFMEPP Design Memorandum**.

**Underlined at bottom:** "Input needed to determine effective applications for MIL-PRF-16173F corrosion preventive compounds."

This is the Class-8-Persistent material the Day-1 audio referenced (clip 5 / Matt Chu's "MIL-PRF-16173 huge conversation" with Mark Ingle) — now made concrete with the technical definition and the SURFMEPP design-memorandum hook.

### Slide (unnumbered) — "Published MIL-PRF-24712B, with Amendment 1, Powder Coating Specification Update"

(`p_mil-prf-24712b-amendment-1.jpg`)

**ISSUE:** MIL-PRF-24712B was published on **14 Feb 2014.** NAVSEA had to update test methods, color, and performance requirements. Interim amendments require full specification update within two years.

- "Performance" specification defines corrosion-control performance requirements for thermosetting powder coatings.

**WAY AHEAD:** NSWC-Carderock updated specification, **SEA 05P2 vetted content,** and final document approved by Specification Review Board for publication.

**RESULT:** **Published MIL-PRF-24712B, with Amendment, on 28 Dec 2025** and provided letter to powder coating manufacturers explaining process for updating qualified product listing on ASSIST website https://quicksearch.dla.mil on **25 Mar 2026.**

- Updated color requirements from cancelled FED-STD-595 to current, active **SAE, AMS-STD-595**. Colors cited include: Bulkhead Equipment Gray, Brown, Gloss; Buff/Coast Guard Spar; OSHA Safety Purple (piping and valve marking); etc.
- Cited current NAVSEA haze gray (≈26270) color defined by Commission Internationale del'Eclairage (CIE) LAB color space to match (i.e., ΔE < 0.5) MIL-PRF-24635 tristimulus values for NAVSEA haze gray.
- Updated toxicity requirements to current ACGIH and Navy and Marine Corps Force Health Protection Command citations.

Bottom statement: "**MIL-PRF-24712B, with Amendment 1, publication supports timely powder coating procurement.**"
