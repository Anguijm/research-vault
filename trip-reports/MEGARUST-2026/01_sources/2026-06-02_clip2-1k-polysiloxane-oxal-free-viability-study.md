# NSRP SPC Panel — 1K Polysiloxane Oxal-Free Coatings Viability Study Presentation (clip 2)

## Summary

A ~14-minute project briefing immediately following clip 1 (start times 12 minutes apart suggests these are sequential presentations within the same NSRP SPC Panel morning session). Eric (Elzly Technology, project lead) presented an early-stage Viability Study of **1K (single-component) Oxal-free polysiloxane topcoats meeting MIL-PRF-24635F Type V Composition 1** in operations-environment (OE) shipyards. The study is being executed in partnership with **HII Ingalls, HII Newport News, BAE Jacksonville, and NCP Coatings** — three Navy ship-repair yards plus the coating manufacturer. Scope: identify knowledge gaps comparing 1K to traditional 2K polysiloxane systems on production rates, waste, performance, and rework. 12-month project, kickoff six weeks ago, demos planned in the fall, final report February 2027. Q&A surfaced questions about Oxal-vs-no-Oxal performance comparisons (already covered by NRL), 1K shelf-stability after partial use (Glenn from NCP confirmed prior Oxal-containing version was "extremely shelf-stable" for 30+ days after open), primer stack-up comparison (under consideration), novel mixing-free packaging (will be mentioned in the report), and water-borne coatings (out of scope — would exceed the period of performance).
