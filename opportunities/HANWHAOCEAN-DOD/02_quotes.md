## Rear Adm. Neil Koprowski, Commander, U.S. Naval Forces Korea

> "The Republic of Korea's ability to conduct large-scale maintenance to USNS ships within the Indo-Pacific Theater demonstrates the strong strategic partnership between the Republic of Korea and the United States. Maintenance in Theater reduces downtime and costs, while enhancing operational readiness. This is a landmark achievement to be celebrated as a symbol of our strengthened partnership and ironclad commitment to the ROK-U.S. alliance."

— navy.mil news story on USNS Wally Schirra ROH completion, 13 March 2025. Source: https://www.navy.mil/Press-Office/News-Stories/Article/4118860/. Verified 2026-06-07 (headed Playwright capture of live page). Tier: 1.

## Cmdr. Patrick J. Moore, Commanding Officer, MSC Office-Korea

> "Hanwha addressed extensive deterioration and damage to the hull, propeller, rudder, and rudder post/steering gear. Notably, Hanwha engineers reverse-engineered the damaged rudder, completely replacing the unit when blueprint were not available. This saved significant time and resources in getting Wally Schirra back to sea, a testament to their resilient supply chains, advanced automations, and skilled workforce."

— navy.mil news story, 13 March 2025. Source: https://www.navy.mil/Press-Office/News-Stories/Article/4118860/. Verified 2026-06-07. Tier: 1. (Note: "blueprint were not available" is the source's exact wording.)

## David Kim, CEO, Hanwha Philly Shipyard

> "We're already in discussions with the Navy [for] different opportunities. There is a lot of support because if you're following what's going on with the Navy and shipbuilding: crisis. Falling behind the number of ships versus the Chinese Navy. Every project [is] behind at least one to three years and that number [is] getting longer. Every project being over budget. And so the Navy needs capacity."

— Remarks to reporters during a Hanwha Philly Shipyard visit (July 2025), reported by USNI News, 27 August 2025. Source: https://news.usni.org/2025/08/27/south-korean-shipbuilder-to-invest-5b-in-philadelphia-shipyard. Verified 2026-06-07 (Playwright capture). Tier: 3. (Bracketed insertions are USNI's.)
