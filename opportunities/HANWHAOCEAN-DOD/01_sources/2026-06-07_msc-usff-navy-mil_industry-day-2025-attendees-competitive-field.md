## Summary

The official attendee roster for the MSC-FLCY 2025 Industry Day (Korea +
Singapore) — i.e., the field of shipyards pursuing U.S. Navy/MSC ship-repair work
in the Pacific. Confirms this is a yard market; Hanwha Ocean is one of many.
Downloaded as an Excel file via headed Playwright.
