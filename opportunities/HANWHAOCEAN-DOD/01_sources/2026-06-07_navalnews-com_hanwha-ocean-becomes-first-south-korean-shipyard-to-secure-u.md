Publication date: August 29, 2024. Author: Naval News Staff.

Hanwha Ocean was awarded a contract to overhaul a 40,000-ton U.S. Navy dry cargo and ammunition ship — the first time a South Korean shipyard secured a U.S. Navy MRO contract. The vessel is serviced at Hanwha Ocean's Geoje shipyard, including comprehensive maintenance, inspection, and onshore overhaul using the facility's floating capabilities.

Agreements and timeline:
- Master Ship Repair Agreement (MSRA) signed July 22, 2024 with the U.S. Navy's Naval Supply Systems Command (NAVSUP).
- Application period of seven months (January–July 2024), shorter than the typical certification period exceeding one year.
- Five years of eligibility to bid on U.S. Navy MRO projects.

Named officials / quotes:
- Carlos Del Toro, Secretary of the U.S. Navy: "Hanwha's acquisition of Philly Shipyard is a game-changing milestone in our new Maritime Statecraft." [VERBATIM UNVERIFIED — confirm against source]
- A Hanwha Ocean official noted the global ship MRO market is "expected to exceed USD 60 billion annually." [VERBATIM UNVERIFIED]

Supporting context: the award followed Hanwha Ocean's acquisition of Philly Shipyard and received backing from South Korea's Ministry of National Defense and Defense Acquisition Program Administration (DAPA).
