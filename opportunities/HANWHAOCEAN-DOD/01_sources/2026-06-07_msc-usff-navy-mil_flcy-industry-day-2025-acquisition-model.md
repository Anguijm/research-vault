## Extracted content

### Contracting authority and demand structure
- NAVSUP Fleet Logistics Center Yokosuka (FLCY) provides contracting support for Military Sealift Command (MSC) ship-repair requirements within the Commander, U.S. Pacific Fleet (COMPACFLT) AOR.
- MSC Ship Support Unit (SSU) requires: Voyage Repair (VRA, quarterly, pier-side, ~14 days); Mid-Term Availability (MTA, every 18 months, 45–60 days); Regular Overhaul (ROH, dry dock, every ~2.5 years, 60–120 days); Service Order (SOR); and continuous maintenance.
- MSC overview: ~130+ ships; five area commands incl. MSC Far East; missions = Combat Logistics Force, Service/Command Support, Special Mission, Prepositioning, Sealift. HQ Norfolk, VA. Welcome letter signed by Mr. Neil Lichtenstein, MSC Deputy Director of Ship Management.

### Recent changes (2025) — the entry barrier dropped
- MSC requirements are now solicited through **full and open competition** to ALL potential offerors in the AOR, posted on **AsiaNeco (asia.neco.navy.mil) and SAM.gov**. FLCY is discontinuing courtesy email notices.
- **USNS platforms no longer require a Master Solicitation or MSRA/ABR.** USS platforms still require MSRA/ABR. (So the MSRA Hanwha earned in 2024 is no longer strictly required for the USNS auxiliary work — the gate is lower than the press narrative implies.)

### How awards are made
- Contract type: **Firm-Fixed-Price** for all requirements. Place of performance: contractor's facility or Navy ports in the AOR.
- Solicitations over the Simplified Acquisition Threshold (>$250K) posted 30 days.
- Evaluation (Section M): Price; Technical (Acceptable/Unacceptable — Factor 1 Understanding the Work Package, Factor 2 Ability to Perform, Factor 3 Management Control & Quality Assurance); Past Performance (Acceptable/Unacceptable, within past 3 years); MSRA/ABR (USS only).
- **Award basis: Lowest Price Technically Acceptable (LPTA).**
- Certified Cost or Pricing Data required >$2M; Cost Accounting Standards compliance >$7.5M OCONUS; active SAM.gov registration required. CPARS performance reporting for contracts >$500K.

### Current MSRA/ABR holders in the AOR (as of 27 Jan 2025)
- South Korea: 3 MSRA, 3 ABR. Japan: 4 MSRA, 19 ABR (incl. Sasebo, Okinawa). Singapore: 2 MSRA, 5 ABR. India: 3 MSRA, 1 ABR. Philippines: 2 MSRA, 2 ABR. Plus Thailand, Australia, Sri Lanka. Totals: 16 MSRA, 33 ABR holders.

### Annual Submittals Program (the standing entry mechanism)
- Lets shipyards submit technical documentation for approval once per year (via an FLC Request for Information, expected ~Jun/Jul); an approval letter (valid 1 year) then lets a contractor reference it instead of resubmitting technical factors on every bid. Voluntary. Covers subfactors such as Material Control & Storage, Heavy/Cold Weather Plans, Fire & Flooding Protection, Facilities, Quality Control Plan, Hazardous Waste Management.

## Notes

- Strategically decisive for the CACI question: this is an **LPTA, FFP, full-and-open shipyard market** — price-driven steel/labor competition among Pacific yards. There is no services/IT/C5ISR insertion point in the MRO *contracting* itself. Any CACI angle must be government-side (supporting FLCY/MSC) or out-of-scope of this market entirely.
- The contracting office (NAVSUP FLC Yokosuka) is the operator's own SRF-JRMC-adjacent world.
