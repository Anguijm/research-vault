## Summary

MSC's official FY2025 annual report ("2025 In Review"), 33 pages, downloaded via
headed Playwright from the 403-blocked MSC site. The decisive passage frames the
Hanwha Ocean / Korea overhaul as a deliberate strategic step, not a one-off:
MSC's own stated *vision to broaden in-theater ship maintenance in the
Indo-Pacific*. Also documents MSC's maintenance-hub structure and the per-area
maintenance cadence.

## Extracted content

**The strategic-intent passage (pages 9 and 26):**

> "USNS Wally Schirra successfully completed a dry-docking Regular Overhaul (ROH)
> period at Hanwha Ocean Co, Ltd. Shipyard repair facility in Geoje, Republic of
> Korea. This ROH was a significant building block for realizing the Navy's
> vision to broaden the scope of ship maintenance and repair work in the
> Indo-Pacific region."

> (March entry) "Dry cargo ship USNS Wally Schirra completed a seven-month
> regular overhaul (ROH) at Hanwha Ocean, Gyeongsangnam-do, Republic of Korea.
> The ROH marks the first time that a Republic of Korea shipyard bid on and won
> an ROH contract of this scale for an MSC vessel."

**Maintenance structure (page 3):** "Ship maintenance and support functions are
integrated into six maintenance hubs under the Area Commands" — locations include
Naples, Italy; Manama, Bahrain; Singapore; **Yokohama, Japan**; and others.

**Footprint (page 6):** MSC Far East is headquartered in **Singapore (Sembawang)**;
area-command offices/representatives include **Okinawa, Japan; Republic of Korea;**
Diego Garcia; Pearl Harbor; and others. MSCO Korea is an MSC office.

**Maintenance cadence benchmark (page 23):** MSC Europe & Africa "and regional
shipyard partners completed $32 million in maintenance evolutions including one
Regular Overhaul Dry Dock, two Mid-Term Availabilities and nine Voyage Repair
periods" plus 27 emergent Casualty Reports — mirrors the FLCY ROH/MTA/VR cadence
and gives a per-region maintenance-spend benchmark.

**Leadership (page 27):** "MSC's leadership in the Far East changed hands when
Capt. David L. Reyes relieved Capt. Robert R. Williams as commander of MSCFE at
the Singapore Naval Installation in Sembawang."
