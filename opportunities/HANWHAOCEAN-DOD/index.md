## Working summary

This is an **exploratory research sandbox**, not a brief-pointed folder. Hanwha
Ocean is a South Korean shipbuilder active in US Navy-relevant work; this folder
exists to determine *whether and how* it represents an opportunity for CACI. The
operator deliberately did not state a working hypothesis at scaffold time —
finding the hypothesis is the first job of the research.

The role Hanwha Ocean plays is undetermined by design. Candidate roles, per the
scaffolding interview, are: a **customer** we could sell capability to, a
**teaming partner** (prime or sub), and/or **customer-adjacent intel** to the
operator's existing Pacific ship-repair (Ship Repair Facility and Japan
Regional Maintenance Center, SRF-JRMC) world. A competitor lens is considered
the least likely. The eventual buyer is likewise open — Hanwha itself, a
Navy/government activity with Hanwha as the executing yard, or none (if this
resolves to pure landscape intel).

The eventual deliverable, if the research warrants one, is a **one-page
executive brief** for a defense pure-play exec. That is the *eventual* output,
not a near-term target. Sensitivity is **shareable**. Nothing is out of scope
yet; scope narrows as the research surfaces it.
