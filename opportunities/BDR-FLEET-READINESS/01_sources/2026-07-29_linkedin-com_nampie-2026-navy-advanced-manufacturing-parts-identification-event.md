## Facts of record (all attributed to the posts, not independently verified)

- **NAMPIE = Navy Advanced Manufacturing Parts Identification Event.** First one since 2021, so a
  five-year gap. Started on or about 17 July 2026 per the operator.
- Organised and led by **CNRMC**, hosted at **SIMA Norfolk**.
- Participants: Active and Reserve Engineering Duty Officers, **NSWC Philadelphia**, **NSWC
  Carderock**, **NAVSEA 05**, the **Submarine Industrial Base (SIB)**, **NAVSUP Weapon Systems
  Support (WSS)**.

  readiness.

## Why this matters here

**Assessment. It lands on a RAND recommendation the vault is already building on.** RAND RR-A470-9
found that poor availability of necessary parts might cause significant delays in wartime repair,
and recommended the Navy reconstitute critical parts and a skilled workforce. NAMPIE is CNRMC
identifying exactly those parts and routing them to advanced manufacturing. The Navy is acting on
that recommendation, at the parent command of the regional maintenance centers, and it started six
weeks after RAND published.

**Assessment. The named participants overlap the BDR brief's cast.** NSWC Carderock is the modelling
source in that brief's original framing; NAVSEA 05 is named there as the data-release technical
authority for hull, mechanical and electrical data. Both are in the room at NAMPIE.

**Assessment. SIMA Norfolk is the Atlantic counterpart to a stand-up already tracked.** SIMA San
Diego stood up 4 June 2026 under a December 2025 CNO mandate, per the MegaRust Day-3 keynote, which
also noted the same restructuring running at MARMC on the Atlantic side and flagged that a new
contracting footprint would emerge. NAMPIE is being used to "inform and shape SIMA's future advanced
manufacturing capabilities", which is a capability being defined right now.

**Assessment, and the strongest single thread.** Advanced manufacturing with reverse engineering as
its weak point is exactly what SWRMC's executive director described at MegaRust, and it is the
operator's own team's area. NAMPIE is the enterprise-level version of the same problem.

**Speculation, flagged.** Whether NAMPIE creates any procurement is unknown. Nothing here indicates
contractor participation, and the named participants are all government. A five-year gap followed by
a restart could equally mean a revived programme or a one-off.
