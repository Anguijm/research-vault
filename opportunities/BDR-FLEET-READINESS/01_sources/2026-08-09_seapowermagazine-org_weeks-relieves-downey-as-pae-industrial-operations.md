## Facts of record

**FACT.** Rear Adm. Todd Weeks relieved Vice Adm. James P. Downey as Portfolio Acquisition Executive
Industrial Operations on 6 August 2026, serving as **interim** PAE IO.

**FACT.** Rear Adm. Pete Small now commands Naval Sea Systems Command.

**FACT.** The previously dual-hatted arrangement has been **separated**: NAVSEA command and PAE
Industrial Operations are now distinct positions held by different officers.

**FACT.** Downey is slated to retire after 40 years of service.

**FACT.** Weeks previously served as Director of In-Service Submarines and Industrial Base, with
prior flag assignments as Program Executive Officer for Strategic Submarines and for undersea
warfare systems.

## Why it matters

**Assessment. The separation is the story, not the name change.** As of 20 July 2026, per USNI News,
one officer held both NAVSEA and PAE Industrial Operations. Splitting them is a structural step in
the same realignment that moved roughly 70 percent of systems-command functions and workforce into
the Portfolio Acquisition Executives. It is consistent with the comprehensive Navy Systems Command
study being underway, and it is evidence the restructuring is progressing rather than stalling.

**Assessment.** "Interim" is doing work in that sentence. An interim PAE IO suggests the final
structure is not settled, which reinforces the risk already recorded in BDR capture brief section
8.7: an office briefed today may not hold the decision when a task order is written.

**Assessment.** The vault's engagement targeting is unaffected in substance. PAE Industrial
Operations still oversees maintenance and the public shipyards, and PAE Maritime's Ship Readiness
portfolio is still the named intersection of maintenance and training systems. Only the incumbent
and the dual-hat have changed.

that it would "go stale faster than anything else in the wiki." It went stale in eleven days, and the
