# CACI Response — Sources Sought / Request for Information
## Marine Design and Engineering Services (Notice 3059-9900)
### Puget Sound Naval Shipyard & Intermediate Maintenance Facility

*This response provides the narrative answers requested in the questionnaire. Company data fields are provided separately.*

---

**Experience with marine design and engineering services (Question 2).**
We provide marine design and engineering support to the U.S. Navy across the ship and submarine lifecycle, including directly relevant work at a sister naval shipyard. Since 2017 we have held the engineering-support contract at Portsmouth Naval Shipyard — the Navy's East Coast submarine overhaul shipyard — supporting the Ships Availability Planning and Engineering Center (SHAPEC) and the Deep Submergence Systems Program. That work runs on the Navy's SeaPort Next Generation (SeaPort-NxG) contract vehicle, falls under the same engineering-services classification (NAICS 541330) as this requirement, and continues under a current task order that extends through 2030. We also perform ship design to Navy standards and maintain a naval-architecture and marine-engineering practice spanning hull, mechanical, and electrical systems. This is the same family of work the draft Performance Work Statement describes for Puget Sound.
