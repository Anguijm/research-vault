## 1. Working summary (analyst view)

The question this track answers: **is there a credible CACI role in the PSNS & IMF
Marine Design and Engineering Services IDIQ (Notice 3059-9900), and if so, prime or
sub/teaming?** This is naval-engineering / planning-yard services work — naval
architecture and multi-discipline engineering, ship checks, drawing development,
work packaging, and technical documentation for surface ships and SSN/SSBN
submarines [s.2026-06-11-mdes-pws]. It is a specialized marine-design lane, not
CACI's core C4ISR/SETA footprint, so the working posture is "more likely sub/teaming
than prime" until the past-performance research says otherwise (§7).

## 3. Demand signal — what the customer is asking

### 3.1 Stated requirement (FACT, from the PWS [s.2026-06-11-mdes-pws])

- **Issuer / sponsor:** PSNS & IMF **Code 200 (Engineering Department)**; deliverables
  reviewed/approved by the Commander PSNS & IMF or designee; work under the
  cognizance of the **Chief Engineer, PSNS & IMF**.
- **Why the contract exists:** PSNS & IMF keeps a lean Government engineering
  workforce and uses contracted Marine Design & Engineering services to absorb
  workload surges in ship repair and conversion.
- **Scope:** comprehensive engineering for all current/former U.S. naval vessels —
  naval architecture, civil, structural, mechanical, electrical, electronics,
  industrial, environmental engineering; planning and estimating; designs and
  calculations; material specifications; kitting; troubleshooting and failure-mode
  analysis; assessments/inspections; testing; CAD drafting and modeling; computer
  programming; technical document preparation. **Planning-yard requirements:** ship
  checks, drawing development, Ship Alteration Record (SAR) and Ship Change Document
  (SCD) development, Ship Selected Record (SSR) upkeep, and fleet technical support.
- **Surface AND submarine:** governed by the General Specifications for Overhaul of
  Surface Ships (GSO) **and** the General Overhaul Specifications for Deep Diving
  SSN/SSBN Submarines (DDGOS), plus ABS rules — submarines are explicitly in scope,
  including combat systems and work packaging "subs and surface."
- **Place of performance:** ~90% Kitsap County, WA; ~10% San Diego, CA and Norfolk,
  VA; possible Guam, Hawaii, Far East, Middle East, Mediterranean.
- **Hard local-presence requirement:** full-service office in Kitsap County, WA
  within **30 calendar days** of award.
