## Working summary

This track tests whether there is a credible role for CACI in the PSNS & IMF
Marine Design and Engineering Services IDIQ Multiple-Award Contract (Notice ID
3059-9900) — prime or sub/teaming. Early research (2026-06-11) moved the read toward
**prime-credible** (see research file §6–§7): CACI has analogous submarine-
modernization planning/engineering past performance and a Kitsap County office. The opportunity is a planning-yard / naval-engineering services contract:
naval architecture and multi-discipline engineering, ship checks, drawing
development, work packaging, and technical documentation for U.S. Navy surface
ships **and SSN/SSBN submarines** [s.2026-06-11-mdes-pws]. The discriminator the
Government keeps returning to is documented **submarine** experience
[s.2026-06-11-mdes-qa1].

The work runs on two tracks that stay tethered to each other: (1) research the
CACI capability fit and the competitive / teaming landscape; (2) feed a 25 July
Sources Sought response that answers the actual RFI questionnaire. **Standing rule
for this folder: the hypothesis and any draft response must remain continuously
aligned to the Sources Sought PWS, the RFI questionnaire, and the Q&A documents in
`01_sources/`** — they are the spec, not background.

The load-bearing falsifier to test is the **submarine-modernization past-performance**
gate. A second visible requirement — a **full-service office in Kitsap County, WA
within 30 days of award** [s.2026-06-11-mdes-pws] — is **confirmed already met**: CACI
operates a Silverdale, WA (Kitsap County) office [s.2026-06-11-caci-jobs-silverdale],
so local presence is a CACI advantage rather than a barrier. The three predecessor-
contract incumbents are named in the PWS (see the research file, competitive
landscape).
